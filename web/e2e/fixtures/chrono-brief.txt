CHRONO — 21-SCENE BRIEF
Title: Chrono — A Visual History of Timekeeping
Duration: 90 seconds
Aspect: 16:9
Style: Editorial, kinetic typography, warm palette

SCENE 1 — Cold open. Black to a single ticking second hand against parchment.
Caption: "Time was once a question."

SCENE 2 — Sundial in a Roman forum, shadow sweeping across stone hours.
Caption: "Rome measured it with light."

SCENE 3 — Egyptian water clock dripping into a basin, ripples expanding.
Caption: "Egypt measured it with water."

SCENE 4 — A monk in a candlelit scriptorium marks tallies on vellum.
Caption: "Monasteries measured it with prayer."

SCENE 5 — Cut to a medieval church tower. The first mechanical clock strikes.
Caption: "1283 — Dunstable Priory. The mechanical age begins."

SCENE 6 — Iron escapement clicking, gears engaging in extreme close-up.
Caption: "Verge and foliot. Accuracy: ± 15 minutes a day."

SCENE 7 — Renaissance workshop, brass clockwork on a wooden bench, candles flicker.
Caption: "Galileo dreams of pendulums."

SCENE 8 — Huygens 1656 — first pendulum clock. Wide pendulum sweeps.
Caption: "Pendulum: ± 10 seconds a day. A hundredfold leap."

SCENE 9 — Stormy Atlantic. A wooden ship pitches. Voiceover: "Longitude is killing sailors."
Caption: "1714 — The Longitude Act."

SCENE 10 — John Harrison's workshop. Hands assemble the H4 marine chronometer.
Caption: "Harrison H4: 5 seconds across an ocean."

SCENE 11 — Industrial revolution. Pocket watches roll off assembly lines.
Caption: "Time becomes mass-produced."

SCENE 12 — Train station platform, station clocks synchronized, schedules posted.
Caption: "1883 — Standard Time Zones."

SCENE 13 — Wristwatch on a pilot's wrist, biplane in WWI dogfight.
Caption: "War puts time on every wrist."

SCENE 14 — Bulova workshop, first quartz wristwatch — Accutron 1960.
Caption: "Quartz: ± 1 minute a year."

SCENE 15 — Seiko Astron 1969 — the quartz revolution begins in Tokyo.
Caption: "The watch becomes a microchip."

SCENE 16 — Cesium atomic clock at NIST. Blue glow, oscillating cloud.
Caption: "1967 — The second is redefined: 9,192,631,770 oscillations of cesium."

SCENE 17 — GPS satellite array against starfield. Lines of timing data orbit Earth.
Caption: "GPS is a clock that flies."

SCENE 18 — Optical lattice clock, strontium atoms suspended in laser light.
Caption: "Optical clocks: 1 second in 15 billion years."

SCENE 19 — Smartphone notifications cascade — meetings, alarms, reminders.
Caption: "Time is now everywhere."

SCENE 20 — A child watches a sundial in a museum, exhibit lights warm.
Caption: "And still — we ask the same question."

SCENE 21 — Black. A single ticking second hand. Logo fades in.
Caption: "Chrono. Time, told."
